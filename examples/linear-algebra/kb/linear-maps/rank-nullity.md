# Rank-nullity theorem

## Summary
For a linear map T from a finite-dimensional V, dim V = dim ker T + dim range T. Axler calls this the fundamental theorem of linear maps. The proof extends a basis of the kernel to a basis of V and shows T sends the added vectors to a basis of the range. (agent draft)

## Terms
- rank :: dim range T
- nullity :: dim ker T
